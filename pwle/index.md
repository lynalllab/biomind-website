<p style="font-size: 1.08rem; line-height: 1.7; margin-bottom: 1.5rem;">Mental health biomarker data is currently fragmented across many resources and secure environments, making it difficult to assess how genes and molecules affect mental health. The Open Psychiatry Project changes that.</p>

<h2 style="margin-bottom: 2rem;">Help us to shape this project</h2>

<h3 style="margin-bottom: 0.4rem;">What you'd be part of</h3>
<p style="margin-bottom: 2.5rem;">We have divided the main tasks into 3 different projects, running across the life of the award.</p>
