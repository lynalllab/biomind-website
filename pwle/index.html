Publications Projects Team Optibio Research Lived experience

Mental health biomarker data is currently fragmented across many resources and secure environments, making it difficult to assess how genes and molecules affect mental health. The Open Psychiatry Project changes that.

Help us to shape this project

What you'd be part of

We have divided the main tasks into 3 different projects, running across the life of the award.

2027
PROJECT 01

Conversations with science

Helping AI to unlock the biology of mental health for public understanding.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

You will help us decide what people actually want to ask about the biology of mental health, test early AI prototypes, and define what a "good, trustworthy, and understandable answer" looks like.

2027–2028
PROJECT 02

Clues in the records

Deciding what matters most in hospital & GP data.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

You will review types of real-world health data (from prescription histories to hospital admissions) and help prioritize which specific measures are meaningful enough to invest time in unlocking.

2029
PROJECT 03

The Co-Pilot Study

Leading a team-based mental health analysis, with questions driven by lived experience.

WHAT YOU WILL DO

You will join a collaborative team to pitch research ideas, design a custom study protocol, and test run the secure analysis on the resource.

Contact openpsychiatryproject@medschl.cam.ac.uk to find out more and how to register. Thank you for your interest.
