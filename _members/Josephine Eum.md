Josephine is a PhD student in the Department of Psychiatry, funded by Mental Health Research UK – Fieldrose Charitable Trust. She holds a BSc in Biomedical Science from King’s College London and a Master’s degree in Systems and Synthetic Biology from Imperial College London. Prior to commencing her PhD, she worked as a research assistant at the Francis Crick Institute, where she investigated the role of gamma delta T cells in cancer.
