Yuzhuo is a PhD student in Psychiatry at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Dr Mary-Ellen Lynall and Dr Xueyi Shen. Her research uses multi-omics approaches to investigate the biological mechanisms underlying psychiatric disorders, particularly depression, and explore their potential clinical relevance. Outside the lab, she enjoys playing badminton and tennis, rowing, boxing, and practicing meditation.
