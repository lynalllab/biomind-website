Shuxian Xu is a Research Laboratory Technician in the Lynall Lab. She holds a PhD in Psychiatry and has a background in both clinical psychiatry and biomedical research. Her research interests focus on neuroinflammation and the role of extracellular vesicles in psychiatric disorders. Outside the lab, Shuxian enjoys travelling and hiking.
