Sirui is an MPhil student in Medical Science (Psychiatry) at the University of Cambridge, supervised by Dr Mary-Ellen Lynall and Ed Hutchings. She graduated from King’s College London with a BSc in Neuroscience and Psychology. Her current research uses genetic approaches to explore how inflammation relates to structural and functional brain networks and mental health. Outside of research, she enjoys travelling and film photography.
