Alicia is a postdoctoral researcher in the Lynall group. She uses multi-omic data to study how the genetic regulation of the transcriptome and proteome varies across biological contexts, and how this contributes to psychiatric outcomes and transdiagnostic phenotypes such as fatigue.  

Alicia completed her PhD in the Department of Psychiatry at the University of Oxford, where she investigated polygenic and pharmacogenetic contributions to antidepressant treatment outcomes. 

She now looks beyond genomics to the biological state patients are in before treatment begins, with the long-term aim of identifying biomarkers to guide treatment prognosis and selection. 

Outside the lab, she can be found at run club, on the tennis court or in the pool, and loves travelling, camping and hiking, though she still misses the beaches back home in Australia.
