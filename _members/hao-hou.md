Hao is a postdoctoral researcher who joined the Lynall Lab in 2026. He holds a PhD in psychiatry from Wuhan University in China and is also familiar with research approaches in public health and nursing. Hao is interested in epidemiology, particularly large prospective cohort studies using publicly available datasets and integrating multi-omics data. 
His previous research focused on the epigenetic mechanisms linking childhood experiences to depression. More recently, he has become interested in how infections become biologically embedded and influence the risk of mental illness. He is currently exploring how the risk of depression changes over time following infection, as well as genome-wide association studies (GWAS) of longitudinal infection patterns. Outside the lab, Hao enjoys modifying electric motorcycles and dreams of travelling around the UK on one. 
He also loves playing League of Legends and reached Challenger rank on the Chinese server.
